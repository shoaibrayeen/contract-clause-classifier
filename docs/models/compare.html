Where they agree and disagree

On the full test split (10,000 provisions) the two models give the same top-1 clause type for 94.2% of provisions. When they agree they are right 94.9% of the time, so agreement is a strong confidence signal. On the 580 provisions where they disagree, the transformer is right 52.8%, the classical model 25.5%, and both are wrong 21.7%. At least one of the two is right for 93.9% of provisions.

Which to use

Transformer for the highest accuracy and for unusual or paraphrased wording, since it reads meaning rather than matching words. Classical when speed, cost or explanations matter: it is about 128× faster per provision on a CPU, a fraction of the size, trains in minutes, and every prediction can be traced to phrases. Both together (the playground's compare mode, POST /v1/compare): when they agree, the answer is very likely right; when they disagree, the provision deserves a human look.
