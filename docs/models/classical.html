Classical machine-learning model. Each provision becomes a sparse TF-IDF vector of word phrases and character fragments, and a linear classifier (logistic regression) scores the 78 clause types. No neural network, CPU only, and every decision can be traced to weighted phrases. Latest version: ledgar-tfidf-lr-v2.

Training: candidates and loss

Every candidate is trained on the same 60,000 provisions and the same features, and scored on the full validation split. Log loss is the cross-entropy of the predicted probabilities (lower is better); a large gap between training and validation log loss means over-fitting.
